CC3: Policy Debate

Has reducing US imprisonment rates been accompanied by rising crime, undermining the case for continued decarceration?

Imprisonment rates fell from 506 to 360 per 100,000 between 2008 and 2023 — a sustained, two-decade decline in incarceration.

Violent crime fell over the same period, from 463.6 to 385.6 per 100,000 — moving with imprisonment rates, not against them.
